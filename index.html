I am a Senior UX Designer with strong expertise in research and B2B product design. I have over seven years of experience in UX, user research, and product analytics, working in regulated industries where the users are experts and the margins for error are small.

Currently at Clarivate, working on Derwent Innovation, a patent search platform used by analysts, IP counsel, and R&D teams globally.

How I work

I started in data, as a master data analyst at TD Synnex working in SAP, before moving into UX. That background still shapes how I approach design: I want to understand the numbers, not just the stories people tell in interviews.

At Roche, I worked on diagnostic workflows in a highly regulated environment. That taught me how to design for expert users who don't have time to tolerate bad interfaces.

Outside of day-to-day design work, I have spoken at UX Brighton and UX Nordic on the challenge of building trust in AI-powered products — something I think about a lot.

Process Initiative
Feature Health Dashboard (FHD)
Clarivate · Cross-functional

Identified a gap in how the department evaluated feature performance and took ownership of solving it — designing and establishing a repeatable quarterly process from scratch, in collaboration with a product analyst.

  • Defined the methodology and built the full scoring framework: feature adoption rate, usage depth score, success rate, and friction signals (error clicks, dead clicks, rage clicks).
  • Designed a composite Feature Health Score (weighted: adoption 20%, quality 40%, depth 40%) and a 3×3 assessment matrix categorising features as Core Assets, Growth Opportunities, Critical Quality Issues, or candidates for audit/sunset.
  • Turned Pendo data exports into a structured, repeatable quarterly process — giving UX, product, and analytics a shared language for roadmap decisions.
  • Gained cross-team buy-in as a department-wide standard.
